About Omni Minerals

Built for a
Stronger Tomorrow.

Omni Minerals is a Zambian-owned company connecting mineral commodity trade, industrial mineral supply and equipment solutions with the needs of mining and industrial operations.

We work with mining companies, mineral processing plants, contractors and industrial organisations, providing dependable materials and operational support.
