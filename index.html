About Omni Minerals

Built for a
Stronger Tomorrow.

Omni Minerals is a Zambian-owned integrated mining support and industrial supply company serving the mining sector through reliable mineral commodity trade, industrial mineral supply, strategic procurement and equipment solutions.

We partner with mining companies, mineral processing plants, contractors and industrial organisations, providing the materials, equipment and operational support that modern mining operations depend on.
