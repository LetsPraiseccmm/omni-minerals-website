Cookie Policy

What browser storage this website uses and how to manage it.

Last reviewed: 22 September 2026

Essential storage

This website stores your cookie preferences locally in your browser so that it can remember whether you accepted or rejected optional categories. This is necessary for the preference control to work and is not used to build an advertising profile.

Analytics and marketing

No analytics, advertising, marketing, social widgets, maps, video embeds or other optional tracking scripts are installed on this website at present. Analytics and marketing preferences therefore remain disabled even when a user selects “Accept All” until a future service is actually added and disclosed.

Third-party cookies

There are no third-party embeds or tracking scripts intentionally loaded by the current website. External links such as WhatsApp, email and telephone actions may take you to services with their own privacy and cookie practices.

Managing preferences

Use the control in the footer to reopen the preference interface. You can also delete local website data through your browser settings. Blocking all storage may affect preference remembering, but the public pages remain available.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the website adds or removes services. Questions may be sent to info@omniminerals.co.zm.
